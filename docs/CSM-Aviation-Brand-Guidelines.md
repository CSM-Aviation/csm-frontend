## 01 · Brand at a Glance

CSM Aviation is a Central Valley aviation partner built on trust, understated quality, and a calm sense of command.

The primary lockup pairs the **CSM** monogram in Cormorant Garamond with a fine horizon rule and the word *Aviation* beneath — the rule a quiet nod to flight, lending the mark its sense of calm, level balance. Always reproduce it from approved master artwork; never re-typeset it.

### The Voice

Refined and considered. We speak plainly and never oversell — the tone of a trusted operator: precise, reassuring, quietly proud. Safety and seamlessness lead every message.

### The Look

Deep Petrol anchors the system, warmed by Saddle and lifted by a Burnished Gold accent. Cormorant Garamond carries display moments; Inter handles working text. Whitespace is structural.

### The Rules

- One decisive accent at a time.
- A 70/25/5 color balance.
- Generous clear space around the mark.

These standards keep every touchpoint — card to hangar — recognizably CSM.

| Variation | Usage |
|---|---|

Use the reversed mark on Deep Petrol wherever possible — it is the preferred expression. On light grounds, use the positive Petrol mark. Single-color black is reserved for situations that cannot reproduce color.

## 03 · The Block Lockup

The **block lockup** sets the mark inside a solid container — a self-contained, high-contrast unit for wherever the logo must hold its own against a busy, photographic, or unpredictable background.

### Approved Block Colors

| Container Color | Mark Color | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Petrol** | Fog | **Preferred** |
| **Saddle** | Fog | — |
| **Burnished Gold** | Deep Petrol | — |
| **Fog** | Deep Petrol | — |

### Avoid

- Don't alter the block's proportions; the container is a fixed ratio.
- Keep the mark and container to the approved color pairings only.
- Don't crowd the mark inside the block; the internal padding is part of the artwork.
- Don't place a block on a same-color ground, where the container disappears — use the open mark instead.

## 04 · Clear Space & Sizing

### Clear Space

Keep a minimum margin equal to the **cap-height of the C** (1×) on all sides of the lockup. Nothing — type, imagery, edges, or other logos — should intrude on this zone. When in doubt, give it more.

### Minimum Size — Print

The lockup should never appear narrower than **0.9 in / 23 mm** wide. Below this, the *Aviation* letterspacing closes up and legibility suffers.

### Minimum Size — Screen

On screen, hold a minimum width of **90 px**. For favicons and app marks, the standalone **CSM** monogram may be used without the *Aviation* line.

> *Clear-space and minimum-size standards proposed by Hundred10 — adjust if production constraints require, but never reduce clear space below the 1× cap-height rule.*

## 05 · Logo Misuse — What Not to Do

| ✕ | Rule |
|---|---|
| ✕ | **Don't stretch or distort.** Always scale proportionally. |
| ✕ | **Don't recolor.** Use only approved block colors. |
| ✕ | **Don't rotate.** The horizon line stays level. |
| ✕ | **Don't crop or crowd.** Respect the block's built-in padding. |
| ✕ | **Don't add effects.** No shadows, glows, or bevels. |
| ✕ | **Don't substitute fonts.** Never re-typeset the mark. |

## 07 · Color in Use — Balance & Usage

### The 70 / 25 / 5 Ratio

| Proportion | Color | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **70%** | Fog | Calm ground — let it dominate. |
| **25%** | Deep Petrol | Structure, headers, and brand blocks. |
| **5%** | Burnished Gold | A single decisive accent, never a field. |

This restraint is what makes the brand feel premium.

### Approved Pairings

- Fog on Petrol
- Petrol on Fog
- Gold on Petrol

### Accessibility

- Fog on Deep Petrol and Deep Petrol on Fog both clear **WCAG AA** for body text.
- Avoid Burnished Gold or Horizon as text colors on Fog at small sizes — reserve them for large display or non-text accents.

## 08 · Graphic Elements — Striping & Cheatlines

A restrained striping system extends the brand beyond the logo. Drawn from aviation's tradition of the fuselage cheatline, the bars add a finished, considered edge to any surface.

### The Edge Bar

A single solid bar along one edge — as used on the business card and crew badge. It grounds a layout without competing with the mark.

- **Default color:** Saddle
- **Alternates:** Burnished Gold or Deep Petrol where the palette calls for it.
- **Width:** Hold the bar to roughly **4–6%** of the surface's shorter dimension.

### The Cheatline (Paired Bars)

Two parallel bars — a heavier **Saddle** bar above a fine **Burnished Gold** bar — set close together. A direct nod to traditional aircraft striping. Run it horizontally across a panel or wrap a vehicle, signage, or aircraft. Keep the weight ratio near **2 : 1**.

### Do

- Keep bars parallel to the surface edge.
- Use one striping device per surface.
- Pair the Saddle and Burnished Gold cheatline on Deep Petrol for the most premium read.

### Don't

- Don't angle, taper, or curve the bars.
- Don't stack more than two.
- Don't let striping crowd the logo's clear space or overpower the layout.

## 09 · Typography — The Typefaces

### Display & Headlines — Cormorant Garamond

**Weights:** Regular · Medium · SemiBold · Italics

A classical serif with modern proportion. Used for display sizes, headlines, and brand moments — set tight (−0.025 to −0.03em) at large sizes.

### Support & Working Text — Inter

**Weights:** Light · Regular · Medium · SemiBold

A neutral, highly legible sans for body copy, labels, captions, contact details, and any small-size or UI text. Letterspaced uppercase Inter handles eyebrows and labels.

---

## 10 · Type in Use — Hierarchy

### Hierarchy

| Level | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | Cormorant Garamond Medium | Tracking −0.025em |
| **Subhead** | Cormorant Garamond SemiBold | — |
| **Body copy** | Inter Regular | 1.55 line-height |
| **Eyebrow / Label** | Inter SemiBold | Tracking 0.22–0.28em · Uppercase |

### Do

- Pair tight Cormorant headlines with calm Inter body.
- Keep one display idea per layout.
- Let italics in Burnished Gold carry the single accent moment.

### Don't

- Don't set body copy in Cormorant; it's a display face.
- Don't letterspace Cormorant or tighten Inter labels.
- Don't introduce a third typeface.
- Don't stack multiple gold accents in one view.
